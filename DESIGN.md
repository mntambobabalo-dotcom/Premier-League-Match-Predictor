# Matchroom

This is a personal football analysis tool. Lead with the fixture and its three-way
probability strip. Use chalk white text on a deep plum scoreboard with pale yellow
controls. A cyan draw segment makes the three-way strip readable alongside its
text labels. Give prices tabular numerals and put fair odds next to entered odds.

The runtime tokens live in the :root block in web/index.html. Controls use shared
native selects, dates and numeric fields. Forms display inline status and keep
entered values on errors. On narrow screens the slip follows match analysis in
document order. Browser storage holds paper selections and the stake.
